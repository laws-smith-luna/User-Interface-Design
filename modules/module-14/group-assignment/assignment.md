# Final Project Presentation and Writeup

**Course:** SWE 632 — User Interface Design and Development
**Module:** 14
**Type:** Group Assignment (Final Project — 10% of overall course grade)

---

## Purpose

In this assignment, you will produce an engaging, written report to document your team's work on the course project. You will summarize your iterative design processes and document key deliverables in each phase of your project. Ideally, this deliverable will be something that you can build on for your professional portfolio when seeking jobs. You will also present your final prototype and a comprehensive summary of your design process in a 10-minute presentation.

## Instructions

This assignment consists of 2 deliverables:

**Final Report.** This final written project report will give you a chance to synthesize your findings from each stage of the design thinking process and reflect on the evolution of your project throughout the semester. You can reuse content from previous deliverables, but this written piece should read as though written for a public audience. Make sure the report is cohesive, engaging, and understandable for someone unfamiliar with your project.

### Suggested Outlines

Your final report should follow the below outline using separate sections for the top-level items.

1. Project name & value proposition

2. Team member names and roles

3. Problem/solution overview (2-4 sentences)

4. Needfinding

   a. Interviews: Who did you interview? How did you recruit & compensate participants (if applicable)? How did you interact with these participants? etc.

   b. Synthesis: How did you synthesize? What did you learn?

5. Design evolution

   a. Final solution

      i. Description

      ii. Rationale for the selected solution, grounded in evidence

   b. Tasks

      i. Describe your tasks

      ii. Describe why each task is important to your user base

      iii. Annotated task-flows using images of the final interface

   c. Design evolution visualization(s) and rationale

      i. Images of the major UI iterations in your project

      ii. Include annotations/captions to explain flows and evolution as necessary

      iii. For each iteration explain evaluation technique, what was learned, and how the design changed in response (implications)

6. Final prototype implementation

   a. Tools used; pros and cons of these tools

   b. Wizard of Oz techniques used (if any)

   c. Hard-coded techniques used (if any)

7. Reflection & next steps

   a. What were your main learnings from this course about the design thinking process and your own project?

   b. If you had more time, what might you add in the future?

---

**Final Presentation.** Prepare a 10-minute presentation that describes your system to those unfamiliar with your domain. Introduce the problem space, briefly mention the user needs analysis you performed and how the process you employed helped you arrive at your final system design and prototype. The focus of this presentation should be your proposed system; what advantages it has over the current state of affairs and what problems it solves. Make sure to mention the iterative design you used for your design and development, all the phases of user research, expert reviews, and usability testing that helped you arrive at your final design. You should also demonstrate how your high-fidelity prototype works, via playing a demo video of your design or performing a live demonstration of it. All group members should contribute to creating your presentation.
