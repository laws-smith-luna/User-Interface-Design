# SWE 632 — Essentials

## Norman (M2) — most important
- **7 Stages:** Goal → Plan → Specify → Perform → Perceive → Interpret → Compare
- **2 Gulfs:** Execution (intent→action) · Evaluation (state→understanding)
- **7 Principles:** Affordances, Signifiers, Mappings, Feedback, Constraints, Conceptual Model, Discoverability
- **Affordance** = what's possible. **Signifier** = visible cue of where to act.
- **Slip** = right goal, wrong action. **Mistake** = wrong goal/plan.

## UCD & CI (M3–M4)
- **Double Diamond:** Discover · Define · Develop · Deliver
- **CI** = watch users *in context*. Observe behavior, don't ask hypotheticals.

## Prototyping & Usability (M5–M6)
- **Sketches** explore *right design*; **prototypes** refine *design right*
- **4–5 participants catch ~80% of issues**

## Site Design & Interaction (M7–M8)
- **Fitts's Law:** bigger + closer = faster. Screen edges = infinite target.
- **Universal design** (curb cut) vs. **assistive** (equivalent action)

## Errors (M9)
- Unintended: **Slip** (attention) / **Lapse** (memory)
- Intended: **Mistake** (rule/knowledge) / **Violation**
- Best fix: **prevent with constraints** > show fix > detect
- **Forcing function:** ATM gives card before cash

## Visual Design (M10)
- **Gestalt:** Proximity, Similarity, Continuity, Closure, Area, Symmetry
- Hierarchy via **bolder/lighter, not bigger/smaller**

## Icons & Viz (M11–M13)
- **Shneiderman's Mantra:** Overview → zoom/filter → details on demand
- **Tufte:** maximize data-ink, kill chartjunk
- **Effectiveness:** position works for all data types

## Community Design (M14)
- **4 Challenges:** Start · Contribute · Commit · Regulate
- **3 Commitment types:** Affective (want) · Normative (ought) · Need-based (must)
- **6 Dark Patterns (SUMSOF):** Sneaking · Urgency · Misdirection · Scarcity · Obstruction · Forced action

## When stuck
Which gulf? Which mental model? What's invisible? What constraint is missing?
